Geometric Algebra Game

This is a small game I created using Geometric Algebra (GA) rather than the traditional Matrix mathematics. The goal of the game is to hit the green targets and avoid the red targets.

I used GA for instance to create a local coordinate system, to make the ball bounce in the scene, and to make a camera with a spring arm.
